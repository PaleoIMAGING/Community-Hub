# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The PaleoIMAGING community is committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, respectful, and productive environment for everyone participating in its activities.

We welcome participation regardless of career stage, discipline, institutional affiliation, nationality, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, disability, age, ethnicity, religion, or other personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and professionally;
- engage with ideas and evidence rather than attacking individuals;
- welcome questions and contributions from people with different levels of experience;
- provide constructive scientific and technical criticism;
- respect different disciplinary perspectives and methodological approaches;
- acknowledge sources, ideas, and contributions appropriately;
- avoid disruptive, intimidating, discriminatory, or harassing behavior;
- respect privacy and confidentiality when information is not intended to be public.

Scientific disagreement is welcome. Personal hostility is not.

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- harassment, discrimination, or intimidation;
- personal insults or derogatory comments;
- deliberate disruption of discussions;
- unwanted sexual attention or inappropriate personal conduct;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- sustained bad-faith participation, trolling, or inflammatory behavior;
- plagiarism or deliberate misrepresentation of another person's work or statements.

## Scientific discussion

PaleoIMAGING encourages open scientific debate.

Disagreement about methods, interpretations, standards, datasets, or scientific conclusions should be expressed clearly and supported with evidence where possible.

Criticism should focus on the scientific or technical issue, not on the individual making the contribution.

## Moderation

Community maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content that violates this Code of Conduct or substantially disrupts productive discussion.

Depending on the severity or recurrence of the behavior, maintainers may also restrict or remove a participant's ability to contribute to the community spaces.

Moderation decisions should aim to protect constructive participation and the integrity of the community.

## Reporting concerns

If you experience or observe behavior that may violate this Code of Conduct, please contact the PaleoIMAGING maintainers privately.

A dedicated contact address or reporting mechanism may be added here by the PaleoIMAGING coordination team.

Reports should be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to PaleoIMAGING GitHub spaces and to community interactions directly associated with PaleoIMAGING activities, including online discussions, meetings, workshops, and collaborative activities.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is informed by widely used open-source and scientific-community conduct guidelines, including the Contributor Covenant, and has been adapted for the PaleoIMAGING community.
