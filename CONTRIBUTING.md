# Contributing to the PaleoIMAGING Community Hub

Thank you for contributing to the **PaleoIMAGING Community Hub**.

This repository is primarily a community space. Contributions may take the form of discussions, questions, shared resources, suggestions, documentation improvements, or proposals for community activities.

## How to participate

### Start or join a Discussion

GitHub Discussions is the preferred place for:

- scientific and technical questions;
- methodological discussions;
- instrumentation and workflow topics;
- data formats, metadata, FAIR principles, and interoperability;
- collaboration opportunities;
- working-group discussions;
- publications, datasets, protocols, and useful resources;
- announcements and community activities.

Before starting a new discussion, please check whether a similar topic already exists.

Use a clear and informative title so that others can easily understand and find the discussion later.

### Ask questions

When asking for help, provide enough context for others to understand the problem. Where relevant, include:

- the imaging technique or instrument;
- the type of sample or dataset;
- software or data format involved;
- the workflow step where the problem occurs;
- relevant references, screenshots, or example files when appropriate.

### Share resources

Community members are encouraged to share publications, datasets, protocols, software, standards, training material, and other relevant resources.

Whenever possible, include:

- a stable link or DOI;
- a short explanation of why the resource is relevant;
- licensing or access information when applicable.

### Propose changes or community initiatives

Suggestions for new activities, working-group topics, standards, recommended practices, or documentation are welcome.

For substantial proposals, starting with a GitHub Discussion is recommended so the community can comment before any formal change is made.

## Issues and Pull Requests

Issues may be used for specific, actionable tasks related to this repository.

Pull Requests may be used for concrete changes to repository documents such as the README, contribution guidelines, community documentation, or other maintained files.

For broad scientific or community discussions, use GitHub Discussions instead.

## Scientific conduct

Contributors should:

- distinguish evidence from interpretation;
- cite relevant sources when making substantive scientific claims;
- acknowledge uncertainty where appropriate;
- avoid misrepresenting the work or statements of others;
- respect intellectual contributions and appropriate attribution.

## Language

English is the preferred language for public community discussions and repository documentation, in order to maximize international participation.

## Community standards

All participation must follow the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

By contributing, you agree to engage constructively and respectfully with other members of the PaleoIMAGING community.
