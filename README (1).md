# PaleoIMAGING Community Hub

Welcome to the **PaleoIMAGING Community Hub**.

This repository supports the community activities of **PaleoIMAGING** and serves as the source repository for GitHub Discussions. It is intended as an open space for researchers, students, technical staff, developers, and other participants interested in imaging approaches for paleoscience and related Earth science applications.

## Purpose

The Community Hub provides a place to:

- discuss methods, instrumentation, and workflows;
- ask questions and share practical experience;
- exchange information about data formats, metadata, FAIR principles, and interoperability;
- share publications, datasets, protocols, software, and other resources;
- identify opportunities for collaboration;
- support PaleoIMAGING working groups and community activities;
- announce meetings, workshops, training events, and other relevant opportunities.

## Discussions

Most community interaction takes place through **GitHub Discussions**.

Suggested discussion areas include:

- **Announcements**
- **General**
- **Questions & Help**
- **Methods & Instrumentation**
- **Data, Metadata & FAIR**
- **Working Groups**
- **Collaborations**
- **Resources & Publications**

When starting a new discussion, please choose the category that best matches your topic and use a clear, descriptive title.

## Participation

Participation is open and contributions from researchers at all career stages are welcome.

Before contributing, please read:

- [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)
- [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

The aim is to maintain a constructive, inclusive, and scientifically useful environment.

## About PaleoIMAGING

PaleoIMAGING is a community focused on imaging techniques, data integration, interoperability, and related methodologies in paleoscience and Earth science research.

This repository is intended primarily as a **community space**, rather than as a software-development repository.

---

If you are new to the community, consider introducing yourself in the Discussions area and briefly mentioning your institution, research interests, and imaging techniques or datasets you work with.
